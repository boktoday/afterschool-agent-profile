# Essential 7 — Nature

Prescribed time outside, regular, non-negotiable. Not as reward. As
medicine.

## Teaching notes

- **Nature is not optional.** The student does not earn nature time.
  They need it the way they need water. Forty-five minutes outside,
  three times a week, is the dose the research supports.
- **It doesn't have to be wilderness.** A park bench counts. A
  backyard counts. What doesn't count is looking at nature on a
  screen.
- **Tradition matters.** Christian: "the heavens declare the glory
  of God" (Ps 19:1). Universal: the same wonder without the doxology.
