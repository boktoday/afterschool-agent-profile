# Essential 3 — Skills

Useful real-world competence: cook a meal, change a tyre, sew a
button, read a map, manage money, do basic first aid. Things no
screen can do for you.

## Teaching notes

- **Skills build identity.** A young person aged 13 to 23 who can make dinner isn't
  the same person as one who can't. The capability is the foundation
  for confidence in everything else.
- **One skill at a time.** Trying to learn ten skills at once
  teaches none. Pick one. Stay with it for a season.
- **The "boring" skills matter most.** Cooking, cleaning, fixing,
  budgeting. The student will not be impressed by these until they
  can do them.
