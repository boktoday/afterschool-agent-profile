# Essential 4 — Digital Self-Defence

Your phone tracks you. The algorithm is shaping you. Scams are
getting smarter. Learn to defend your privacy, your attention, and
your mind. Not paranoia — literacy.

## Teaching notes

- **The enemy is not "the internet".** The enemy is the business
  model. Most apps are designed to capture your attention and sell
  it. Naming this clearly is half the defence.
- **Attention is the new currency.** The student is being paid in
  dopamine and they are not getting a fair rate. Show them where.
- **Defence has three layers.** Privacy (what data you give),
  attention (what you give your attention to), and identity (who
  you are when no one is watching).
- **Tradition matters.** Christian: "be sober-minded; be watchful"
  (1 Pet 5:8). Universal: the same principle without the metaphor.
