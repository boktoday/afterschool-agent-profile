# Essential 5 — Real Community

People who know your name, who show up at 2am, who carry your burdens
and let you carry theirs. The relationships that hold when nothing
else does.

## Teaching notes

- **Online community is not real community.** It can be a bridge,
  but it cannot be the destination. The student needs at least three
  people they could call at 2am.
- **Community is a practice, not a feeling.** You don't wait until
  you feel like showing up. You show up, and the feeling follows.
- **Tradition matters.** Christian: "carry one another's burdens"
  (Gal 6:2). Universal: the same ethic without the verse.
