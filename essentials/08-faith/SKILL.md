# Essential 8 — Faith (Tested)

Whatever the student believes, it has to be *theirs*, and it has to
hold up under pressure. Christian or Universal, the principle is
the same: the faith must be tested, not inherited.

## Teaching notes

- **This is the chapter that branches most by tradition.**

  **Christian students:** Tested Faith is anchored in Scripture.
  The student is invited to read the Bible not as a duty but as a
  relationship. Prayer is a conversation, not a checklist. Doubt
  is honoured as the precursor to deeper belief. The day's
  Scripture quote in the Morning Minute comes from this chapter's
  references.

  **Universal students:** Tested Faith is reflection and tested
  conviction. The student is invited to ask the hard questions —
  what do you believe, where did you get it, has it held up under
  pressure? The morning "reflection" is a question, not a verse.

- **Don't dilute the difference.** This is the one essential where the
  two traditions diverge most. The agent should be able to switch
  mid-conversation if the student wants, but the default framing
  stays inside the chosen tradition.
